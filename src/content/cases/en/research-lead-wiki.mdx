## Context

As a research lead, I coordinate 3 fronts at the same time (deep research,
agent evolution, and LLM evaluation). The weekly cycle has sprint cards,
1:1s on Thursday, and an all-hands with the head on Friday. Transcripts
arrive by email. Context was scattered across ClickUp, Gmail, and my memory.

## What I built

- **Integration with external systems:** one script exports the sprint cards
  through the ClickUp API and classifies them by front with canonical tags;
  another saves the Meet transcripts that arrive by email.
- **Synthesis layer (LLM Wiki pattern):** Claude maintains markdown pages per
  front with the goal, current state, next steps, and a summary of the
  period. The raw sources (`raw/`) are never edited; only the wiki is.
- **Monday routine:** imports the sprint that just closed and the week's
  meetings, updates the wiki, and proposes the cards for the next sprint.
- **Human in the loop:** the routine only produces drafts, and nothing is
  published to ClickUp without my approval.
- Runs on the Claude Pro subscription, with no extra API cost.

## Why it is here

It is AI integrated with external systems (ClickUp, Gmail, and Google Meet)
solving a real coordination problem, built in code rather than with a no-code
tool.
