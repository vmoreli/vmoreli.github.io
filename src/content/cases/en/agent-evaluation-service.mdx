## Context

The company builds agentic products on top of a base of shared services.
Each new agent needs to be checked for risk before it
reaches a client, and the choice of model drives its cost.

## What I am doing

- I scoped the problem and the literature (agent evaluation, LLM-as-a-judge,
  extracting test cases from traces) and designed the service as one more of
  the company's shared services.
- **Evaluation harness:** a guided process that reads the code of a LangGraph
  agent, understands its scope and tools, and builds test cases together with
  the developer. The risk focus is hallucination, unexpected answers, and
  violations of client-specific rules.
- Design constraints: a target cost of US$ 3–5 per run, human in the loop
  with minimal intervention, and an internal report.
- Custom runner with DeepEval. The pilot is a ReAct agent with read-only
  tools.
- **Building with AI:** the plan goes into my Claude Code skills pipeline
  (spec → implementation → test → review), and I review each step. Step 0
  (contract and ADRs) is done.

## Why it is here

It shows initiative (proposing the service myself), a product view (risk
before the client, model cost), and a way of building with AI while keeping
human control.
